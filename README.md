Entry for [Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990) on [make.fun](https://make.fun) — a one-star review written by a house cat about its human, under 200 characters, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The entry

> One star. She bought me a toy mouse with a bell. I have hidden the mouse. The bell now belongs to me.

A specific, recognisable complaint (the bell toy), deadpan review shape, and the sting lands in the last words — the cat keeps the bell, the human loses.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it with Node 22+:

```sh
node main.ts
```
